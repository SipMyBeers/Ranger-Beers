Overview

What You'll Do

Top-tier leadership in the Infantry. Senior NCOs responsible for the training, discipline, and welfare of large infantry formations.

Initial entry training focusing on core military skills. You will undergo the Red, White, and Blue phases, culminating in the Forge FTX.

No AIT for 11Z
Entry Path
EntryPromotion from a feeder MOS

11Z has no Advanced Individual Training of its own. It is a senior NCO MOS: Soldiers reach it by promotion from a feeder MOS in the same career field, after completing that feeder MOS’s own training and serving in it.

Service in a Feeder MOS
Experience
RequirementsSet by the Army

Which feeder MOSs lead to 11Z, and the promotion requirements, are set by the Army and change over time. Check the current Army MOS classification guidance (DA Pam 611-21) or a career counselor for the specifics.

Award of 11Z
Senior NCO
AwardedOn promotion

11Z is awarded by promotion from a feeder MOS, not on graduating from a school.

Key Responsibilities

  • Maintain unit readiness
  • Execute tactical missions
  • Support branch operations
